<fg=blue;options=bold>Sylius Advanced Taxon Plugin</> is installed.

  <options=bold>Four steps are left:</>

    1. Migrate the database: the plugin extends the taxon, channel and taxon image tables.

       <comment>bin/console doctrine:migrations:migrate</>

    2. Build the plugin assets. The plugin ships the entrypoints <comment>plugin-admin-entry</> and
       <comment>plugin-shop-entry</>, and its templates load <comment>app-admin-entry</> and
       <comment>app-shop-entry</>, so importing them from your own entrypoints is enough:

       <comment>assets/admin/entrypoint.js</comment>
       import '../../vendor/cyllene-digital/sylius-advanced-taxon-plugin/assets/admin/entrypoint.js';

       <comment>assets/shop/entrypoint.js</comment>
       import '../../vendor/cyllene-digital/sylius-advanced-taxon-plugin/assets/shop/entrypoint.js';

       Then rebuild your assets as usual (<comment>yarn build</> or <comment>yarn encore dev</>).
       Without this build, the taxon customizations render but stay unstyled and the icon picker,
       facet conditions and storefront sliders stay inert.

    3. Create the pictogram directory and make it writable: the plugin stores uploaded pictograms
       there and never creates it itself.

       <comment>mkdir -p public/media/advanced-taxon/icons</>

    4. Schedule the conditional taxon synchronization: it keeps the products assigned to
       conditional taxons up to date when products, attributes or stock change afterwards.

       <comment>bin/console cyllene:advanced-taxon:sync-conditional-taxons</>

  <options=bold>One thing to know:</> the plugin routes and the product links it renders use the
  default <comment>/admin</> back office path. If your back office lives elsewhere, the conditions
  preview endpoint and the product shortcuts of the taxon form have to be adapted.

  Then clear the cache (<comment>bin/console cache:clear</>) and open
  <comment>Catalog > Taxons</> in the back office to configure a taxon.

  Documentation:
  https://github.com/CylleneDigital/AdvancedTaxonPlugin/blob/main/docs/installation.md
